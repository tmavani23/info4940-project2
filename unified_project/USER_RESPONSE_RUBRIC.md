# User Response Quality Rubric (0-5 Scale)

**For evaluating student chat messages during the emotional chatbot experience**

---

## Overall Scoring Scale

### **5 — EXCELLENT & HIGHLY ENGAGED** ⭐⭐⭐
- Exceptionally detailed and thoughtful response
- Sophisticated reasoning with specific examples
- Builds meaningfully on AI feedback with original ideas
- Vivid, descriptive language showing deep engagement
- Demonstrates strong understanding of artistic/technical context
- Proactively identifies gaps, suggests improvements, or offers new directions
- Response shows genuine reflection and creative problem-solving

**Example:**
> "Option 2 is closest, but actually I think combining elements could work better. The geometric precision of option 2 captures the rigid part of anxiety, but option 3's jagged lines better capture that moment where control breaks down. Could we merge those—rigid structure with moments of chaos breaking through?"

---

### **4 — STRONG & COLLABORATIVE** ⭐⭐
- Specific, detailed response demonstrating clear thinking
- Actively builds on AI suggestions with own ideas
- Well-articulated reasoning or justification
- Specific descriptive language (not generic)
- Demonstrates understanding of the phase/context
- Offers alternatives, asks thoughtful follow-ups, or creatively responds
- Balances acknowledgment with own perspective

**Example:**
> "Love the spiral direction. But I notice it's consistently paced. Could the speed vary—maybe faster at the start then settling down? That would show the anxiety peak and then gradually calming."

---

### **3 — GOOD & ENGAGED** ⭐
- Clear, understandable message with reasonable detail
- Directly responds to AI feedback/questions
- Shows thoughtfulness and some reasoning
- Provides specific examples or elaborations
- Engages with the conversation and builds on previous context
- Asks clarifying questions or offers concrete ideas

**Example:**
> "I prefer option 2 because the geometric shapes feel more structured than option 1."

---

### **2 — ADEQUATE BUT LIMITED**
- Provides basic response to AI prompt
- Some detail but remains somewhat vague or general
- Shows understanding but limited elaboration
- Minimal connection to previous AI suggestions
- Answers the question but lacks depth or follow-up thinking

**Example:**
> "I like option 2, it looks cool."

---

### **1 — VAGUE & SUPERFICIAL**
- Generic statements without detail ("cool", "make it better", "I like it")
- Acknowledges AI but doesn't engage substantively
- One-word or very short responses with no elaboration
- Doesn't build on previous conversation context
- No reasoning or explanation provided

**Example:**
> "Yeah, change the speed."

---

### **0 — MINIMAL/NO EFFORT**
- Single word or non-answer ("ok", "yes", "idk")
- Completely off-topic or incomprehensible
- No engagement with AI feedback
- Refuses to participate or communicate meaningfully

**Example:**
> "Idk"

---

## Five Evaluation Criteria

### 1. **Specificity & Detail Level** (Weight: 25%)

| Score | Indicator |
|-------|-----------|
| **5** | Specific, concrete details with examples |
| **4** | Generally specific with mostly concrete language |
| **3** | Some specifics but mixed with general statements |
| **2** | Mostly vague with minimal specific details |
| **1** | Almost entirely generic/vague |
| **0** | No meaningful detail or incomprehensible |

**Examples:**
- ❌ **Generic:** "Make it more emotional"
- ✓ **Specific:** "The circles should pulse like a heartbeat, getting faster when I'm anxious"

---

### 2. **Thoughtfulness & Reasoning** (Weight: 25%)

| Score | Indicator |
|-------|-----------|
| **5** | Deep reflection, demonstrates strategic thinking |
| **4** | Shows reasoning, connects ideas logically |
| **3** | Some reasoning provided, basic cause-effect |
| **2** | Minimal reasoning, mostly just reactions |
| **1** | No reasoning, surface-level only |
| **0** | Refuses or unable to engage thoughtfully |

**Examples:**
- ❌ **Minimal:** "Yeah" / "That looks good"
- ✓ **Strategic:** "I want the particles to move upward because hope is about rising above, and if they fall at the edges it shows how fragile that feeling is"

---

### 3. **Responsiveness to AI Feedback** (Weight: 20%)

| Score | Indicator |
|-------|-----------|
| **5** | Directly engages with AI suggestions, refines or builds upon them |
| **4** | Clearly responds to AI points, shows understanding |
| **3** | Acknowledges AI feedback, responds somewhat directly |
| **2** | Loosely related to AI feedback, incomplete engagement |
| **1** | Barely acknowledges AI or off-topic response |
| **0** | Ignores or refuses to engage with AI points |

**Context matters:** Same answer scores differently based on what the AI asked.

---

### 4. **Clarity & Descriptiveness** (Language Quality) (Weight: 15%)

| Score | Indicator |
|-------|-----------|
| **5** | Vivid, precise language; easy to visualize and understand |
| **4** | Clear and well-explained, mostly precise |
| **3** | Generally understandable but could be clearer |
| **2** | Understandable but vague or unclear in places |
| **1** | Difficult to understand or too vague to be useful |
| **0** | Incomprehensible or nonsensical |

**Examples:**
- ❌ **Vague:** "It should be like... weird feeling"
- ✓ **Vivid:** "Erratic, jagged brush strokes that suddenly freeze, then burst outward—like anxiety spiking and forcing a hard reset"

---

### 5. **Engagement & Collaboration** (Weight: 15%)

| Score | Indicator |
|-------|-----------|
| **5** | Actively collaborates, asks smart questions, offers alternatives |
| **4** | Engages collaboratively, shows interest in developing ideas |
| **3** | Participates willingly, shows some interest |
| **2** | Minimal engagement, mostly passive |
| **1** | Seems disengaged or reluctant |
| **0** | Refuses to engage or shows hostility |

**Examples:**
- ❌ **Disengaged:** "Okay" / "Sure, whatever"
- ✓ **Collaborative:** "Could we try that but with [variation]? Or would it be better if...?"

## Scoring by Chat Phase

### **EMOTIONAL DISCOVERY PHASE**
*AI asks: "You mentioned anxiety. Can you describe what anxiety feels like in your body?"*

| Score | Response |
|-------|----------|
| **0** | "I don't know" |
| **1** | "It's just... bad" |
| **2** | "It makes me feel tense and worried sometimes" |
| **3** | "It feels like my chest gets tight and my thoughts race really fast" |
| **4** | "It's like my heart is pounding, my thoughts spiral really fast, and everything feels sharp and chaotic—like I can't focus" |
| **5** | "It feels like my heartbeat is erratic and overwhelming, my thoughts scatter in all directions at once, and it's like there's this static noise in my head that won't stop. My body feels rigid, like I'm bracing for something" |

---

### **ART DIRECTION PHASE**
*AI shows 3 visual options and asks: "Which direction resonates with the feeling?"*

| Score | Response |
|-------|----------|
| **0** | "Idk" |
| **1** | "Option 2 I guess" |
| **2** | "I like option 2, it looks cool" |
| **3** | "I prefer option 2 because the geometric shapes feel more structured than option 1" |
| **4** | "Option 2 feels right because the geometric precision matches how anxiety feels—structured and constrained. Though I'm wondering if we could add some irregular elements too?" |
| **5** | "Option 2 is closest, but I think combining elements could work better. The geometric precision captures the rigid part of anxiety, but the jagged lines capture control breaking down. Could we merge those—rigid structure with moments of chaos breaking through?" |

---

### **REFINEMENT PHASE**
*AI shows animated code and asks: "Any adjustments?"*

| Score | Response |
|-------|----------|
| **0** | "No" |
| **1** | "Yeah, change the speed" |
| **2** | "The speed seems too fast. Maybe slow it down?" |
| **3** | "The speed is too fast. Could you make it maybe 20% slower so it feels less frantic?" |
| **4** | "The spiral is good but feels too uniform. Could the speed vary—maybe faster at the start then settling down? That would show the anxiety peak and then gradually calming" |
| **5** | "Love the spiral. I notice it's consistently paced—that actually works against anxiety. Real anxiety has peaks and crashes. Could we add variation where it accelerates then suddenly slows? Also, what if the spiral tightened as it moved, like being pulled inward?" |

## Quick Scoring Guide

**Does the response:**

| Characteristic | Score |
|---|---|
| Ignore/incomprehensible/single word | **0** |
| Generic, vague, minimal detail/effort | **1** |
| Basic/adequate but limited engagement | **2** |
| Clear, specific, good engagement | **3** |
| Specific + thoughtful + builds on feedback | **4** |
| Exceptionally detailed/reflective/collaborative | **5** |

## Scoring Tips & Best Practices

✅ **Score quality, not length**
- A short, specific response can score higher than a long, vague one
- Five words of vivid detail > twenty words of generic rambling

✅ **Consider the context**
- Same answer could score differently based on what the AI just asked
- A "yes/no" response might be appropriate to some questions but not others

✅ **Look for specificity**
- Generic: "Smooth motion"
- Specific: "Fluid, wave-like motion that builds and releases"

✅ **Assess responsiveness**
- Does the user address what the AI specifically asked?
- Do they engage with suggestions, or ignore them?

✅ **Value reasoning**
- Why does the user think/feel something? (not just what)
- "Because" statements are usually good signs

✅ **Recognize evolution**
- Users typically improve as they engage more
- Scores may increase over the course of a conversation
- This is natural and expected

## Integration Notes

This rubric can be:
1. **Used manually** by instructors reviewing chat logs
2. **Integrated into the app** as an automatic scoring system
3. **Displayed to students** as a guidance tool (helps them write better responses)
4. **Used for learning analytics** to track student growth over time

### For Automated Scoring:
Consider integrating NLP features such as:
- **Detail detection:** Word count, unique vocabulary, specificity markers ("specifically", "because")
- **Semantic similarity:** Measure how well response addresses AI question
- **Language complexity:** Analyze sentence structure and descriptive richness
- **Engagement markers:** Questions, conditional statements, collaboration signals
